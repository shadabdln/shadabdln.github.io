03Projects

Featured work

★ M.Tech Research

Explainable ML for 5-Year Colorectal Cancer Survival Prediction

An end-to-end, reproducible ML pipeline that predicts five-year overall survival for colorectal cancer patients and explains each prediction. Trained on the SEER 17 cancer registry and externally validated on AACR Project GENIE.

View code
257,827patients, SEER 17 registries
0.877test ROC-AUC (95% CI 0.874–0.880) on 50,338 held-out patients
0.878temporal validation AUC (train 2010–15, test 2016–18)
0.790external validation AUC, AACR Project GENIE
0.809best C-index across 5 survival models
+0.042AUC over the stage + age + sex clinical baseline (0.835)

What I did

  • Built the cohort from 324,174 registry records (cleaning, exclusions, feature engineering) down to 257,827 patients.
  • Benchmarked 6 classifiers (Logistic Regression, SVM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM) with stratified 5-fold CV and 1,000-sample bootstrap confidence intervals.
  • Checked calibration (slope 1.02), decision curves and subgroup performance, not just AUC.
  • Compared 5 survival models: Cox PH, CoxNet, Random Survival Forest, Gradient Boosting Survival, XGBoost-Cox.
  • Explained predictions globally and per patient with SHAP, LIME and partial dependence plots.

How it ships

  1. DVCprepare → train → evaluate pipeline with params.yaml
  2. MLflowexperiment tracking and Model Registry on DagsHub
  3. CIGitHub Actions: pytest on synthetic data, no-patient-data guard, Docker build
  4. DockerFlask + Gunicorn app with /predict and /health, Kubernetes manifests
  5. Appcalibrated 5-year survival probability + top SHAP factors per patient
